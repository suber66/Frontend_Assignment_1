Можешь объяснить это требование?
Forms
Each student writes at least one form on one of their own
pages, relevant to the project - booking, feedback, registration, order. It must
contain: method and action attributes, fieldset with legend, labels connected
to their fields by id, and the input types text, email, tel, number and date,
plus a radio group, a checkbox, a select with options, a textarea, the required
and placeholder attributes, a submit button and a reset button. The form
does not send anything yet- there is no server behind it - and you must say
so in a comment inside the form. Server-side handling comes later in the course.
