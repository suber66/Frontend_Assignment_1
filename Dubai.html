About our journey

Discover Dubai gives travelers an opportunity to experience the combination of modern architecture, traditional culture and desert landscapes. Visitors can explore famous landmarks, enjoy luxury shopping and discover traditional Arabian markets. Dubai is home to the Burj Khalifa, the world's tallest building, and the Palm Jumeirah, one of the city's most recognizable man-made developments.

Dubai is one of the most popular destinations in the UAE. Travelers can visit the historic Al Fahidi district, explore traditional souks and experience the desert outside the city. The city also offers modern attractions, large shopping malls and beautiful views of the Arabian Gulf.

The contrast between old and new makes Dubai especially interesting. Visitors can experience traditional Arabian culture while staying in a modern international city. The Palm Jumeirah is another famous attraction, known for its distinctive palm-shaped design.

The official name of the country is the UAE, and Dubai is one of its seven emirates. The city is located on the coast of the Arabian Gulf and is known for its warm climate and impressive architecture.
