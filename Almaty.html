About our journey

Discover the highlights of Almaty, from the breathtaking landscapes of the Zailiyskiy Alatau Mountains to the vibrant atmosphere of the city center. Our tour gives visitors an opportunity to experience nature, culture, and local attractions while traveling comfortably with an organized itinerary. Explore popular destinations such as Medeu, Shymbulak, Kok Tobe, and Panfilov Park, and enjoy spectacular views of the surrounding mountains. Please note that additional personal expenses, meals, and optional activities may not; be included in the tour price. Almaty is often considered one of Kazakhstans most beautiful cities, where modern city life meets spectacular mountain scenery.

About hotel

Kazakhstan Hotel is one of the symbols of Almaty and a landmark of Soviet-era architecture. The building rises 102 meters above the city and was one of the tallest buildings in Kazakhstan when it opened in 1977. Its unusual crown-shaped top was designed to make the hotel recognizable from different parts of Almaty. The hotel is located near Dostyk Avenue, close to the city center, with views toward the mountains. One of its biggest attractions is the panoramic view of Almaty and the Zailiyskiy Alatau Mountains, making the hotel not only a place to stay but also an important part of the city's identity.
