About our journey

Discover Almaty gives travelers an opportunity to experience the combination of nature, culture and modern city life. Visitors can enjoy breathtaking mountain landscapes, explore historical landmarks and try traditional Kazakh food. Please note that additional personal expenses, meals, and optional activities may not be included in the tour price. Almaty is one of the most important cultural destinations in KZ, where modern urban life meets spectacular mountain scenery.

The city has many cultural and historical places that visitors can explore. Some cultural sites in Kazakhstan are connected with UNESCO heritage and cultural programs. Travelers can also enjoy local food, city walks and views of the mountains surrounding Almaty.

About hotel

Kazakhstan Hotel® is one of the most famous landmarks in Almaty and an important symbol of the city's history and architecture. Located near Dostyk Avenue, the hotel offers easy access to the city center, parks, shopping areas, and popular attractions. Guests can enjoy comfortable rooms, restaurants, Wi-Fi, and beautiful views of Almaty and the Zailiyskiy Alatau Mountains. The hotel is a convenient base for visiting Kok Tobe, Panfilov Park, Medeu, and other attractions around the city. One returning guest described the mountain view as absolutely breathtaking.

Traveler Tip

Bring comfortable shoes, a light jacket and some water — Almaty is perfect for walking around the city and exploring the nearby mountains. If you plan to visit Medeu or Shymbulak, check the weather before your trip. This tip can be reused for our city and mountain tours, which is why it lives in an <article>.

Did you know?

Almaty is the largest city in Kazakhstan and is located near the foothills of the Trans-Ili Alatau mountains. The famous Medeu skating rink is situated at an altitude of about 1,691 m above sea level.
