About Us

HT.KZ was founded in 2021 with the goal of making travel more affordable and convenient for people in Kazakhstan. We noticed that more and more Kazakhstanis wanted to travel, discover new countries, and enjoy comfortable holidays, but many faced high prices and difficulties when choosing the right tour. Therefore, we created a service that helps customers find a trip that matches their budget and preferences. We offer affordable prices, carefully selected hotels, and a wide range of travel destinations. Some of our popular destinations include Egypt, Dubai, and Almaty. Before adding a hotel to our catalog, we evaluate its location, accommodation conditions, room quality, food, and tourist reviews. We also cooperate with trusted tour operators, which allows us to get better conditions and offer our customers competitive prices. Over five years of operation, HT.KZ has sent more than 1,000,000 tourists on trips. Today, the company has several offices in major cities of Kazakhstan and continues to grow by expanding its range of destinations and improving its service. By choosing HT.KZ, you get a reliable travel partner, affordable prices, and confidence in the quality of your holiday. Plan your next trip with HT.KZ and set off to discover new experiences!
