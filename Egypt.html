About journey

5 Days · Cairo, Egypt · All-inclusive

Discover Ancient Egypt takes travelers through some of Egypt's most famous historical attractions. The tour includes the Pyramids of Giza, the Great Sphinx, the Grand Egyptian Museum, Cairo's historic areas and a Nile experience. The Great Pyramid of Khufu was the world's tallest structure for about 3,800 years and is the only surviving member of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World. It is estimated to have been completed around 2560 BC.

Discover Ancient Egypt takes travelers through some of Egypt's most famous historical attractions. Visitors can explore the traditional souq markets of Cairo, where they can buy souvenirs and local products.Egypt's ancient history has been explored in many books and documentaries, including Ancient Egypt: A Historical Overview.

Tip

Best time to visit Egypt is between October and April, when temperatures are cooler. Bring modest clothing for visits to mosques and historic religious sites.

Journey Through Ancient Egypt

  1. c. 2600 BCE — The Great Pyramid of Giza was constructed.
  2. c. 2500 BCE — The Giza pyramid complex became an important royal burial site.
  3. c. 1500 BCE — Ancient Egyptian temples became major religious and cultural centers.
  4. 30 BCE — Egypt became a province of the Roman Empire.

About hotel

Pickalbatros Royal Moderna Sharm "Aqua Park"® is a 5-star beachfront resort in Sharm el-Sheikh, booked as part of this package on ht.kz. The resort sits on the first line by the sea with its own sandy beach and an on-site aqua park, and operates on an all-inclusive basis with an outdoor pool. It is offered here as the coastal extension of the trip after the Cairo sightseeing days — guests fly from Cairo to Sharm el-Sheikh to relax by the Red Sea. One returning guest wrote that the sunset view was absolutely breathtaking.
