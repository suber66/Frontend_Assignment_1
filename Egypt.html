About journey

Discover Ancient Egypt takes travelers through some of Egypt's most famous historical attractions. The tour includes the Pyramids of Giza, the Great Sphinx, the Grand Egyptian Museum, Cairo's historic areas and a Nile experience. The Great Pyramid of Khufu was the world's tallest structure for about 3,800 years and is the only surviving member of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World. It is estimated to have been completed around 2560 BC

Journey Through Ancient Egypt

  1. c. 2600 BCE — The Great Pyramid of Giza was constructed.
  2. c. 2500 BCE — The Giza pyramid complex became an important royal burial site.
  3. c. 1500 BCE — Ancient Egyptian temples became major religious and cultural centers.
  4. 30 BCE — Egypt became a province of the Roman Empire.

About hotel

iro Nile View Hotel® is a comfortable 4-star hotel designed for travelers exploring the history and culture of Egypt. Located in Cairo, the hotel offers easy access to major attractions and provides a relaxing place to stay after a day of sightseeing. Guests can enjoy comfortable rooms, breakfast, Wi-Fi, a restaurant, and views of the city. The hotel is a convenient base for visiting the Pyramids of Giza, the Sphinx, historic Cairo, and the Nile River. One returning guest wrote that the sunset view was absolutely breathtaking.
