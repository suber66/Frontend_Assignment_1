Ayana.buyer — your trusted personal buyer

A personal buying agent for genuine brand-name goods from the USA, Korea, Turkey, Italy and Germany delivered straight to you in Kazakhstan.

Who we are

ayana.buyer is a personal buying-agent service that helps people in Kazakhstan purchase genuine items from abroad. We arrange the purchase and delivery of goods from the USA, Korea, Turkey, Italy and Germany: clothing, shoes, electronics, accessories and gift certificates. As of September 2026, the project’s Instagram page has 5 994 followers and 347 posts — client stories, brand reviews and ordering instructions.

Larger purchases may be paid in installments through Kaspi (one story mentions a 24-month plan for a Dyson device; availability is confirmed in your quote), and group orders run in a dedicated WhatsApp chat, where clients combine shipments to save on delivery. The project is run by Aigerim Yerbolatkyzy, who personally answers questions in Instagram direct messages and the chat.

How it works

Working with us comes down to three steps:

  1. You send a product link through Instagram direct messages, the WhatsApp chat or the question form.
  2. We calculate the purchase cost, shipping and, if you like, a Kaspi installment plan.
  3. The item travels to Kazakhstan and is handed to you.
