Terms worth knowing

Personal buyer (byer)
A person or service that purchases an item abroad on your behalf and arranges delivery.
Kaspi installment
Paying for a purchase in parts through the Kaspi app. It is confirmed in your quote; this site does not approve it.
Group order
An optional order format where several clients combine shipments in one chat to reduce shipping costs. You can also order alone.
