Terms worth knowing

Personal buyer (byer)
A person or service that purchases an item abroad on your behalf and arranges delivery.
Kaspi installment
Paying for a purchase in parts through the Kaspi app, without going through a bank.
Group order
An order format where several clients combine shipments in one chat to reduce shipping costs.

What an order conversation looks like

The order number you receive in chat looks like this: AYB-2026-0347. A request message template looks like this: 

Hi! I'd like to place a buying request.
Product link: ...
Size/color: ...
Payment: Kaspi installment

After sending the message, press Enter, and the chat usually replies with something like:
