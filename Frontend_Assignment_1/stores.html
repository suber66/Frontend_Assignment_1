Terms worth knowing

Personal buyer (byer)
A person or service that purchases an item abroad on your behalf and arranges delivery.
Kaspi installment
Paying for a purchase in parts through the Kaspi app, without going through a bank.
Group order
An order format where several clients combine shipments in one chat to reduce shipping costs.

What an order conversation looks like

The order number you receive in chat looks like this: AYB-2026-0347. A request message template looks like this: 

Hi! I'd like to place a buying request.
Product link: ...
Size/color: ...
Payment: Kaspi installment

After sending the message, press Enter, and the chat usually replies with something like:

Request received, we'll send you a quote within the day.

Basically, original branded goods are not expensive — it is our market’s markup that is huge. For example, footwear priced at $150–210 in Nike stores in the USA starts from as little as 110,000 ₸ with us. That is why placing an order through a trusted buyer is very worthwhile: first, you get a 100% original product; second, you pay the original (lower) price plus the cost of the buyer’s service; third, American online stores have very frequent and significant discounts.
