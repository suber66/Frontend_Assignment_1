Simple process

How orders are placed

Clients write to us on Instagram direct messages or in the shared WhatsApp group-order chat. The team answers questions about timing and price. Each category page has its own request section.

Order steps

  1. The client sends a product link.
  2. The ayana.buyer team calculates the purchase and delivery cost.
  3. The client confirms the order and chooses a payment method; Kaspi installment may be offered.
  4. The item is delivered to Kazakhstan and handed to the client.
