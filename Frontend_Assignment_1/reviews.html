How orders are placed

Clients write to us on Instagram direct messages or in the shared WhatsApp group-order chat, and the team answers questions about timing and price. As one client put it (translated): From now on we will order through you — the first step is always a cost calculation.

Order steps

  1. The client sends a product link.
  2. The ayana.buyer team calculates the purchase and delivery cost.
  3. The client confirms the order and chooses a payment method, including Kaspi installment.
  4. The item is delivered to Kazakhstan and handed to the client.
